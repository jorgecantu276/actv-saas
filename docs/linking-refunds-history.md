# Verified participants, refunds and attendance

Source candidate **0.2.0-rc.5** builds on the unified operations workspace. It remains unqualified for production. The [readiness record](implementation-readiness.json) continues to track hosted, business, device, legal, operator and cutover acceptance separately from source CI.

Participant ownership requires a confirmed recipient account and an expiring, single-use invitation. A verified online purchaser may invite a participant for an eligible captured order; assigned staff explicitly record their identity-check method and reason. Names, purchaser attribution and cashier attribution never establish personal ownership. Only a digest of the private token is stored. Revocation, expiry, redemption and legacy event adoption are audited. A recipient inbox recovers an invitation after sign-in without storing a raw token. Existing ownership cannot be reassigned by another invitation, and each person has one personal entry per edition. Legacy numeric records retain unknown payment status when linked.

Managers select original order items for a refund; the server fixes the amount from those items and serializes reservations against legacy full-order refunds. Cash refunds require an open shift and an explicit confirmation that the money was disbursed; they become reconciliation movements. Stripe refunds validate the original capture, account, mode, currency, charge and metadata. Lost responses reuse the original request key within the bounded provider window; later recovery performs lookup only. Unknown external partial refunds and disputed payments hold the whole order for review. An explicitly audited participant-scoped refund can preserve unaffected entries. A refund never creates replacement folios or releases capacity. Receipts retain the original captured amount and label adjustments, refunds, holds and entry validity.

Managers can pause, resume, postpone or permanently cancel an edition with a reason, optimistic revision and idempotent request. Postponement changes its operational date and pauses sales. Cancellation is terminal; it blocks new sales, new provider attempts, fresh linking, attendance and kit handover while preserving accepted provider attempts for reconciliation. It does not automatically send a message or refund money. Staff must resolve accepted payments through the original order and record the actual refund result. Original snapshots, folios and reserved capacity remain intact.

Attendance is a separate fact. Original observations cannot be updated or deleted. A manager may append a reasoned correction with its actual timestamp and revision; ordinary check-in cannot bypass a retraction. Effective attendance drives the roster, participant history and optional public achievement. Refunds and event cancellation preserve past observations. Confirmed personal attendance remains in Mi ACTV after eligibility changes. Public sharing is opt-in and revocable, and a retracted attendance is hidden publicly. Retained observations without a known observation date keep their original edition snapshot date; the system does not invent a scan time.

`npm run verify` exercises the bounded SQL and actual Edge functions, then clean and retained-state adoption on disposable local Supabase Auth, PostgREST, Storage and PostgreSQL. The browser suite uses an owned synthetic Chromium session to exercise participant invitation acceptance, attendance correction, refunds and event cancellation. It uses no retained Edge profile, production data, real payment, physical printer or outgoing email. Exact-commit CI and independent review are required before publishing the candidate. Source verification does not qualify hosted configuration, Stripe settlement, actual hardware, approved policies or operator readiness.
